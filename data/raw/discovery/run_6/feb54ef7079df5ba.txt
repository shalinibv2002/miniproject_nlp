Intellectual Property Rights Activity 
 
The Industry Institute Interaction Cell of our college has organized a Patent Awareness Program for 
Students on 17.02.2020 from 10 A.M to 1 P.M at K.S.Auditorium, TCE. The program focuses on how to convert the 
Research and project works into Patents and how to identify innovative and patentable ideas.  250 students have 
attended and benefited with this workshop.
