Comprising ten faculty members and one Tamil faculty member, the department has consistently focused on equipping students to meet job expectations through innovative and effective teaching methodologies. Recognized as a Research Centre of Anna University, Chennai, the department currently offers a Ph.D. program in Language and Literature. In addition to its academic and research pursuits, the department actively organizes various club programs aimed at encouraging student participation, enhancing social interaction, and strengthening communication skills for diverse workplace scenarios, thereby contributing to their development as well-rounded individuals. Programmes Offered The Ph.D. Programme in English offers advanced research opportunities in English Language Teaching, Linguistics, and English Literature. It integrates theoretical and applied perspectives, encouraging interdisciplinary exploration. In English Language Teaching, research focuses on second language acquisition, pedagogy, curriculum design, and technology-enhanced learning. The Linguistics stream examines phonetics, syntax, semantics, sociolinguistics, and discourse analysis, emphasizing language structure, use, and variation. Research in English Literature spans classical to contemporary texts, exploring critical theories, cultural contexts, and emerging fields such as postcolonial and gender studies. The programme fosters critical thinking, analytical skills, and academic excellence through research methodology training, publications, and conference participation. Graduates are prepared for careers in academia, research, and related professional domains.
