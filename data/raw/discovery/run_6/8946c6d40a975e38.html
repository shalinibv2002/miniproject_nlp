Sponsored Research

Objectives

The objective of the sponsored research projects are as follows:

  • To expand the knowledge and develop new technologies or methodologies with practical applications through research.
  • To foster partnerships with the higher learning Institutions and Industries and hence strengthen the research capabilities of faculty, scholars, and students.
  • To increase the number of scholars, high impact factor publications and patents through the sponsored project
  • To improve the TCE research infrastructure

TCE - SEED Money Grant

Recognizing the critical importance of early-stage funding in advancing innovative research and development, this provision establishes a framework for the allocation of seed money specifically for sponsored projects. Sponsored projects, as defined herein, refer to research, development, and innovation initiatives that are supported by external funding sources but require initial capital to reach a stage where they can attract such sponsorship.

This provision is guided by the following principles:

  1. Promotion of Cutting-Edge Research and Development
  2. Attraction of External Sponsorship
  3. Fostering Collaboration and Interdisciplinary Work
  4. Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion
  5. Sustainable and Impactful Investment
