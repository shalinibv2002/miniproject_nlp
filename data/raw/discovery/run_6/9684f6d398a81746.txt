At present, the department offers B.E. Mechatronics and doctoral studies. The department aims at producing talented Mechatronics engineering specialists who cater to the needs of the relevant industries. The faculty members in the department are doing research projects from major funding agencies such as DST, UGC, CVRDE and AICTE. With publications and patents in allied domains, the faculty is equipped with world class skills. The department has full-fledged laboratories to carry out research and consultancy projects in the Mechatronics engineering field. State-of-the art facilities in the laboratories encourage students to carry out research in the areas of Robotics and Automation, Sensors, Internet of Things (IoT), Thermal Management in Engineering Applications and drones. Programmes Offered B.E. Mechatronics Mechatronics Engineering is an interdisciplinary program that combines mechanical, electrical, electronics, computer, and control engineering to design and develop advanced systems and technologies. The program equips students with knowledge in robotics, automation, sensor integration, and intelligent control systems. It emphasizes hands-on learning through projects, labs, and real-world applications. Graduates are prepared to innovate in areas such as industrial automation, robotics, smart manufacturing, and IoT, addressing the needs of modern industries in the era of Industry 4.0 and beyond. Ph.D: The Department of Mechatronics is a Recognised Research Centre of Anna University, Chennai. The Ph.D. programme in Mechatronics Engineering offers a dynamic research environment to explore cutting-edge advancements at the intersection of mechanical engineering, electronics, and intelligent systems. This program focuses on areas such as robotics, automation, IoT, optimization algorithms, control systems, autonomous systems, drone technologies and AI-driven mechatronic solutions. Candidates are encouraged to engage in interdisciplinary research to address real-world challenges in industries like healthcare, aerospace, manufacturing, and automotive. With access to state-of-the-art laboratories and expert faculty guidance, the programme fosters innovation and prepares researchers for impactful careers in academia, and industry.
