TCE-TBI has been operational since 2014 with funding support from the National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB), Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. We cater to the needs of people aspiring to solve a problem by creating a business and facilitating the aspiring students, alumni and entrepreneurs to learn and practice innovation. Vision Nurturing an entrepreneurial ecosystem that supports sustainable startups. Mission Empowering aspiring entrepreneurs, faculty, students, and alumni to transform their ideas into successful startups. Apply For Incubation Click here Our Focus Areas TCE TBI aims to unleash the innovative power of individuals within a vibrant ecosystem that includes a diverse array of advisors, mentors, industry partners, and accredited investors. TCE TBI is passionate about bringing together people with shared dreams and similar values to build sustainable ventures based on innovation and ethics. We provide comprehensive support for innovation, including infrastructure, safety, technical resources, business-related assistance, and creative networking among startups. We facilitate the incubation of innovative startups in the following core areas identified by TCE TBI.
