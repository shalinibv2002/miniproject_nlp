The Indo Universal Collaboration for Engineering Education (IUCEE), with Headquarters at University of MassachusettsLowell, was conceptualized by over 150 leaders of engineering education and businesses from US and India in 2007. The vision of IUCEE is to improve the quality and global relevance of engineering education and research in India and related benefits to US engineering educators, with focus on faculty development, student development, curriculum development, as well as improved teaching technologies & research. For more details: https://iucee.org/teaching-and-learning-centers/ TCE has been collaborating with IUCEE and launching various events since 2016. The Academic Process team of TCE has been actively participating in almost all the programs launched by IUCEE. Any programs initiated by IUCEE are appreciated by TCE Academic Process and appropriate nominations are made to attend the programs. We present a quick travel here under the given sub sections. Anitha – AMCS Outstanding Teaching Learning Center – 2025 ENGINEERING PROJECTS IN COMMUNITY SERVICES (EPICS) @ TCE TCE is a member in Purdue University’s Engineering Projects in Community Services (EPICS) Program. EPICS Program is an engineering-centered, multidisciplinary, community-based approach to design education. EPICS and IUCEE have developed a strategic partnership to expand opportunities for Indian colleges and universities to adapt the EPICS model into institution’s curriculum. We have enrolled in IUCEE-EPICS in August 2017. Prof. Bill Oakes of Purdue University, along with the IUCEE team, offered a formal course in Design Thinking. TCE is recognized as one of the consortium members of IEEE- EPICS-Chennai Chapter. The concept of design thinking throngs the minds of TCE faculty with the IUCEE course on Design thinking. With this seed, a design thinking course has been introduced in TCE as a part of CDIIO framework with the given course outcomes and are able to get number of interesting projects.
