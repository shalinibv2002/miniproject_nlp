Full-time PhD scholars receive robust monthly support, research grants, and special support for international conferences. Benefits: The IndiaAI Research Fellowship provides up to five years of support, including monthly stipends ranging from ₹70,000 to ₹80,000, along with a research grant of ₹2,00,000 per year. Additionally, fellows who get opportunities to present their work on the global stage, putting India’s AI talent firmly on the world map, will be supported by IndiaAI to cover the travel and/or accommodation expenses (up to a maximum of ₹1,00,000/-).
