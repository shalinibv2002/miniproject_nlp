Masters in Engineering (M.E.) are carried out through the Single Window System of the Anna University. Candidates who have written the TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) for M.E. degree programmes/ valid GATE Score are eligible. Students can choose the courses based upon the rank calculated by their marks in the TANCET. Admission to M.C.A. Course is carried out through the Single Window System. Candidates should have passed Bachelor's Degree in 10+2+3 pattern with core Mathematics at 10+2 School level (or) with Mathematics/ Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level. In addition to this, candidates should have appeared for Entrance Examination (TANCET) conducted by Anna University, Chennai- 600025. Students can choose the college based upon the rank calculated by their marks in the TANCET. Reservations are provided for BC/ MBC/ DNC/ SC/ ST etc. as per the rules of the Government of Tamil Nadu. Reservations are also provided for the Physically handicapped/ Ex- Servicemen/ Sports persons/ other state candidates. A Valid TANCET/GATE are prerequisite for admissions to TCE for PG courses.
