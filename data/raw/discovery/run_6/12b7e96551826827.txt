Sponsored projects, as defined herein, refer to research, development, and innovation initiatives that are supported by external funding sources but require initial capital to reach a stage where they can attract such sponsorship. This provision is guided by the following principles: Promotion of Cutting-Edge Research and Development Attraction of External Sponsorship Fostering Collaboration and Interdisciplinary Work Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion Sustainable and Impactful Investment People Head of the TCE SMS Scheme : Dr. L.
