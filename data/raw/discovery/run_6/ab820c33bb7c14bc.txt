CSE (AIML) programme is designed to provide students with in-depth knowledge of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technologies. The program focuses on core computer science courses while integrating advanced topics such as Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Computer Vision. With a foresight to meet industry trends, the programme also emphasizes on courses such as sustainable AI and autonomous agents. Practical learning through hands-on projects, internships, and research opportunities is ensured for industry-readiness. The programme encourages active and experiential learning allowing excellence in AI-driven domains, preparing them for careers in AI development, data science, and intelligent systems design.
