 
 
Revaluation Process/Procedure  
 
The following procedure will be followed instead of  the existing Revaluation 
process after the terminal examinations: 
A student can apply for “Viewing” his/her answer-sc ripts of all the courses 
he/she appeared for theory examinations, except ind ustry supported 
courses, within 3 days including the day of publica tion of results, on 
payment of a prescribed fee as per the procedure no tified by the Controller 
of Examinations in each semester. The student will be permitted to 
appraise the evaluation and make specific appeals on the evaluation to the 
Controller of Examinations. The appeals will be ass essed, by the respective 
Head of the Department and a team of faculty members, and the revision of 
marks, if any, will be updated in the results. 
The above supersedes the Clauses for Revaluation in all UG/PG/PTBE  regulations 
and comes to immediate effect  for ALL  the regular and private  students. 
Procedure outline : 
1. The students have to record, within three days  from the day of publication of 
results, their interest, to take part in the “Answe r-paper Viewing”, through 
online form ,as per the notification made by the Controller of E xaminations 
from time to time, by paying a prescribed fee of Rs . 500/= per subject or 
course. 
2. The students will be allowed, as per the schedule p ublished by the 
Controller of Examinations, to go through their ans wer-scripts. Students 
can record their appeals, if any, in the prescribed  form after viewing the 
marks awarded by the examiner, total marks etc. 
3.  Controller of Examinations will organize to addres s the appeals. Change in 
marks, if any, will be updated in the results and t he revised results will be 
published.
