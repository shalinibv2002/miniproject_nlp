Online Education
1. Does all programs/courses were completed on time. Yes
2. Measures taken to complete the syllabus of courses and programs. Measures taken to complete the syllabus:
The Academic Process team of Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) has taken the responsibility of streamlining online
teaching initiatives. Online classes started in April 2020 in order to complete the remaining portions of even semester 2019-20.
Academic year 2020–21 was started in online mode as per the directions given by AICTE, Government of Tamil Nadu and Anna
University, Chennai.
Following the first phase of online classes, brainstorming was carried out and the following challenges were identified:
•Difficulty in gauging the seriousness and attentiveness of students during online classes
•Lack of Training to online classroom tools for faculty members
•Conduct of Continuous Assessment Tests and  Terminal  Examinations
•Conduct of Laboratory Sessions
The following measures were taken to address these challenges:
I Faculty Development :
External Training:
i.Two week course on “Digital transformation in Teaching Learning Process” was conducted by IITB and 70 faculty members got
certified.
ii.Online Teaching Course organized by Indo-Universal Collaboration for Engineering Education “(IUCEE), was attended by the
Academic Process team members during July –September 2020 and 6 faculty members obtained distinction.
In-house Training:
i.Training in Google Classroom and Canvas was conducted for all faculty members by academic process team during May 2020
ii.FDP on “Developing Learner Centric Online Courses - Resilient Teaching Approach” during August 2020 for 36 faculty members
representing each department. The following topics were covered.
•MOODLE-LMS: Student enrollment, Upload of Course Material, Assignment, Continuous assessment Test, Quiz. A separate
domain www.courses.tce.edu has been created for launching the courses.
•Open source interactive tools (Peerdeck, slido, H5P, mentimeter, polleverywhere, vizia, Quizizz)
•Preparation of video lectures with Screen cast-o-matic
•Pedagogy for online teaching and Assessment with a focus on Instructional design, formulation of multiple choice questions and
rubrics for evaluation
•Follow-up workshops were conducted at each department to equip all faculty members.
II Virtual Lab Classes:
Online laboratory sessions were promoted using virtual labs by serving as a nodal centre in virtual lab initiative by NMEICT.
III    Training on Online Assessment:
Online Assessment Training for creating question banks, quizzes and assignments was conducted in three phases.  A separate
domain of www.exams.tce.edu was created for conduct of semester examinations in Moodle.
IV   Infrastructure Development:
i.Smart boards were installed and staff members were encouraged to use them
ii.Lectures captured by the Lecture capture system in the Institution have been made available to the students to watch and learn
iii.Graphic/pen tablets were procured to have blackboard experience.
  V    Usage of NPTEL, Course era and Edx
i.Collaboration with coursera, NPTEL and edx enabled the students and faculty members to learn and earn certificates.
ii.Credit transfer has been regulated for the NPTEL courses.
iii.Preparation of flexible academic calendar based on students’ needs
VI    Development of TCE online courses
TCE has planned to launch online courses for internal and external students.    Capacity building workshops were conducted. 38
courses are in the process of development as of Feb 2021.
