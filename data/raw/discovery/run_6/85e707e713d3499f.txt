B.E./B.Tech. Regulations 
Clause 4.14 Research Publication 
A student with minimum CGPA of 8.00 without history of arrear can register for the course 
. The outcome of the course will be a publication in SCI/SCI-
expanded/SCOPUS indexed journals in Science and Technology.  Number of Credits for this 
course is 3.  A maximum of two (2) students can form a team and register for this course at 
the beginning of the fifth semester of the programme.  The student shall work under a faculty 
advisor who has published at least 5 papers in SCI/SCI-expanded/SCOPUS indexed 
journals. On producing the evidence of the research publication, through faculty advisor and 
Head of the Department, grade and credits will be awarded in the following semester. The 
grades will be awarded as per the details in the following Table.  
 
S.No Category of Journal Grade  
1 One Research Publication in SCI/SCI-Expanded Journal S 
2 Two Research Publication in SCOPUS indexed  Journals  S 
3 One Research Publication in SCOPUS indexed  Journal A 
 
If the student is not able to publish within seventh semester, the registration will be 
automatically canceled and he/she has to meet the credit requirements for the elective 
category by registering necessary elective course in eighth semester, if required. Further, if a 
student has not registered for this research course in fifth semester but meets all the 
requirements for earning the credits in this course, the student will be allowed to replace 
his/her grade and credits earned from rogramme Specific Elective  or rogramme Specific 
Elective for Expanded Scope with grade and credits earned from this course.
