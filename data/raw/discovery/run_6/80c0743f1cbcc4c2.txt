With a student-centric environment, TCE encourages participation in a wide array of activities, including technical symposiums, sports tournaments, cultural festivals, and student clubs. The lush green campus fosters collaboration and personal growth, supported by well-equipped facilities such as libraries, sports complexes, hostels, and innovation labs. Students actively engage in National Service Scheme (NSS), National Cadet Corps (NCC), and various professional bodies, which nurture leadership and community involvement. Regular workshops, guest lectures, and industry interactions ensure that students are career-ready and socially responsible. The inclusive atmosphere promotes diversity, critical thinking, and holistic development, making TCE not just a place of learning, but a space to grow as an individual. Overall, the campus life at TCE is dynamic, inclusive, and rich with opportunities for intellectual and personal growth. People Dr. G.
