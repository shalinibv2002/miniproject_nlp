The establishment of the CCE will also enable us to leverage our existing academic resources to provide high-quality, flexible education tailored to the needs of learners from different backgrounds. Vision To empower lifelong learners through accessible, innovative, and high-quality educational programs, fostering skill development, professional growth, and societal contribution. Mission We at CCE of TCE shall strive continuously to: Provide diverse and flexible educational opportunities to support continuous learning for individuals across all age groups. Offer industry-relevant programs that equip learners with advanced skills and knowledge for career progression. Encourage a culture of innovation through cutting-edge courses and academic collaborations with higher learning institutions. Address community needs by delivering impactful programs that drive sustainable growth and societal well-being.
