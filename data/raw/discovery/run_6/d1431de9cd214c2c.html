IUCEE


 

TCE is one of the consortium institutions of IUCEE. The Indo Universal Collaboration for Engineering Education (IUCEE), with Headquarters at University of MassachusettsLowell, was conceptualized by over 150 leaders of engineering education and businesses from US and India in 2007. The vision of IUCEE is to improve the quality and global relevance of engineering education and research in India and related benefits to US engineering educators, with focus on faculty development, student development, curriculum development, as well as improved teaching technologies & research.

For more details: https://iucee.org/teaching-and-learning-centers/

TCE has been collaborating with IUCEE and launching various events since 2016. The Academic Process team of TCE has been actively participating in almost all the programs launched by IUCEE. Any programs initiated by IUCEE are appreciated by TCE Academic Process and appropriate nominations are made to attend the programs. We present a quick travel here under the given sub sections.

ENGINEERING PROJECTS IN COMMUNITY SERVICES (EPICS) @ TCE

TCE is a member in Purdue University’s Engineering Projects in Community Services (EPICS) Program. EPICS Program is an engineering-centered, multidisciplinary, community-based approach to design education. EPICS and IUCEE have developed a strategic partnership to expand opportunities for Indian colleges and universities to adapt the EPICS model into institution’s curriculum. We have enrolled in IUCEE-EPICS in August 2017. Prof. Bill Oakes of Purdue University, along with the IUCEE team, offered a formal course in Design Thinking. TCE is recognized as one of the consortium members of IEEE- EPICS-Chennai Chapter. The concept of design thinking throngs the minds of TCE faculty with the IUCEE course on Design thinking. With this seed, a design thinking course has been introduced in TCE as a part of CDIIO framework with the given course outcomes and are able to get number of interesting projects.

INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON TRANSFORMATIONS IN ENGINEERING EDUCATION (ICTIEE)

As part of IUCEE partnership, TCE has been participating in the annual conference of IUCEE - International Conference on Transformations in Engineering Education (ICTIEE) since 2018, held every year in order to facilitate the sharing and collaboration between the institutions, faculty and students associated with IUCEE. TCE has hosted the conference during 2018, 8th & 9th of January 2018. ICTIEE is one of the best forums for sharing of best practices in curriculum design, content delivery and assessment for effective implementation of Outcome Based Education.

IUCEE Student programs

IUCEE emphasis on the implementation of the NEP guideline of providing “holistic and multidisciplinary” education. Support for Fast learners

TCE strives hard to support  fast learners through IIT Bombay Remote Workshops and QEEE programmes.

IIT Bombay Remote Centre (RC 1173)

TCE was recognized as Remote Center of IIT Bombay,  in 2012,  to host the online workshops conducted by IIT Bombay and IIT Kharagpur under  “The T10kT project’’ supported by NMEICT- National Mission  on Education through ICT.  It helps faculty members to constantly upgrade themselves to learn about advances in their domain, and to understand the best practices for facilitating best possible learning, benefitting from each of the programs. Use of online and blended approach allows participants to complete a significant part of training online, thus reducing the time which must be spent on face-to face synchronous interaction.
