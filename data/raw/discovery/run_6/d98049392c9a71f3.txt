Department of Physics in collaboration with other engineering department of TCE offers Ph.D. programme in various specialization such as Smart Materials, Computational Materials, Thin Film Materials and Nano Materials. The Department is supported by FIST programme of DST, New Delhi. The department also collaborates with industries, academic and professional bodies and actively engaged in many technology developments through sponsored projects from BRNS, DRDO, DST, ISRO, CSIR and AICTE.
