 
Intellectual Property Rights Activity- IPR- Designs 
 
As a part of TCE IPR Cell activities, We conducted  a webinar entitled 
"Intellectual Property Rights - Design Patents" on 9th October 2021 (Saturday) 
from 10.30 A.M. to 12 Noon for all our faculty members, research scholars and 
students. This webinar was handled by Mr. Kaja Bantha Navas Raja Mohamed, 
Assistant Professor, School of Mechanical Engineering, Sathyabama Institute of 
Science and Technology, Chennai.  More than 25 faculty members and research 
scholars attended and benefited from this programme.
