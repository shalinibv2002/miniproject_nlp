Description  
TCE, IPR Cell in association with Institute Innovation Council of our college has 
arranged an IPR awareness program under the flagship of KAPILA and NIPAM, a 
government initiative on February 8th 2023.  The resource person of this 
programme is Shri. A. See tha Raman, Examiner of Patents and Designs, Patent 
Office, Chennai.  
The objectives of the programme are to create awareness and educate on 
Intellectual property rights (IPR) among faculty & students, and to increase the 
number of patents. We request all the faculty members to attend the programme. 
Interested students can also attend and get benefitted from the programme.
