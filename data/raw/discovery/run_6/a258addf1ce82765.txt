The pedagogy consists of 3 broad modules. Learning Practice Assessment Learning Module: Students can self-schedule Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Reasoning MCQ (Multiple Choice Question) tests 24/7 and learn using the solution provided. Students can learn C, Java, Python, and C++ from the basics using the Tutorial modules (which provide Hints and Solutions). Debugging programs are provided to enhance the students' error rectification capabilities. Practice Module: The practice programs are categorised under Programming Tracks based on Topics as well as Difficulty Level. Daily 2 challenges are provided for the students to solve. Assessment Module: The training and placement cell of the college can schedule tests (both aptitude and programming). For semester exams, random programs are chosen from a pool (pertaining to the chosen difficulty level) to avoid malpractice. Gamification: Leaderboard provided for all the modules. Internal college contests can be conducted to award Gold, Silver medals based on the performance of the students. Certificates on completion of the modules, which have a QR code and a link that can be shared in portals like LinkedIn. ELITE Programming Training is imparted to good programmers to enhance their skillset in advanced problem-solving techniques.
