This initiative plays a vital role in bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world business practices, enabling students to develop entrepreneurial skills and industry-readiness. TCE E-Cell collaborates with industries for internships, projects, and expert insights, while offering skill development programs and hands-on learning opportunities. These efforts aim to inspire innovation and empower students to turn ideas into successful ventures. The TCE Technology Business Incubator (TBI) provides critical support to the TCE E-Cell by fostering a vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem. It offers infrastructure, mentorship, and funding opportunities to help students and early-stage start-ups turn their innovative ideas into viable businesses. TBI collaborates with the E-Cell in organizing workshops, bootcamps, and networking events while providing access to prototyping facilities like the TCE Makerspace. Additionally, it facilitates connections with industry experts, venture capitalists, and government funding schemes, ensuring holistic support for aspiring entrepreneurs. This synergy enables the TCE E-Cell to nurture innovation and encourage impactful start-up ventures. Vision To cultivate an entrepreneurial mindset and create a thriving ecosystem that encourages innovation, leadership, and start-up creation. Mission To provide a platform for aspiring entrepreneurs to learn, collaborate, and transform ideas into sustainable ventures while building strong ties with industries. Major Initiatives E-Summit: A flagship event bringing together students, entrepreneurs, and industry experts for networking and knowledge sharing. NeN Ignite Program: A structured program focusing on ideation, validation, and early-stage development of start-up ideas, organized for the students and faculty members of TCE E cell Startup Bootcamps: Intensive workshops to help students build and test their Minimum Viable Product (MVP). Business Case Study Completions STAKE – A Mock Business Expo Industry Projects: Collaboration with industries on problem statements to provide practical solutions Design thinking Workshops Achievements Successful start-ups initiated by TCE students with E-Cell and TBI support Recognition at national-level entrepreneurial competitions, Smart India Hackathons and TNSI and StatupTN Contests Internship at IITM RP, Chennai on the cutting-edge transportation projects under the guidance of Prof. Ashok Jhunjhunwala Long-standing collaborations with organizations like NEN (National Entrepreneur Network). The TCE E-Cell continues to inspire and equip students to become entrepreneurs, innovators, and industry leaders, creating a positive impact on society and the economy.
