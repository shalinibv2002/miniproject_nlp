IUCEE NEP Minicourses Details

 

Indo Universal Collaboration for Engineering Education (IUCEE) Mission National Education Policy (NEP) offers Mini courses to improve the quality and global relevance of engineering education. IUCEE emphasizes the implementation of the NEP guideline of providing “holistic and multidisciplinary” education. The NEP mini courses provide unique perspectives to problem-based and project-based learning. Based on teaching strategies, engages participants in considering how these elements could be integrated into their own courses. Nearly 340 students were undertaken these courses till AY 2026
