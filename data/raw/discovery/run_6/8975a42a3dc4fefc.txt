Guidelines for Award of Moderation marks   
 
1. Moderation of maximum 5 marks is given to each semester to cover all the 
theory subjects appeared by the students for the Regular and Supplementary 
Examinations. 
2. Moderation marks will be given only to the subject  requires passing minimum 
marks (Terminal Examination and Total Marks ) as specified in the curriculum 
and not for the improvement of Grade obtained. 
3. Award of such moderation marks will be added only to the Terminal Examination 
marks, not in the Continuous Assessment Marks. 
4. While allotting moderation marks, the subject(s) require less moderation marks 
will be given priority.  
5. If the external marks  obtained are same in two subjects (less than passing 
minimum) among the subjects in a semester, for allotting moderation marks the 
subject(s) having more internal marks will be given priority. If  again, the 
internal marks are same in both the subjects, the allocation of moderation 
marks goes as per the subject order listed in the curric ulum of the respective 
semester/degree programme. 
6. Sl.No. 4 and 5 are repeated as long as the available moderation marks can be 
used for making ‘passes’. 
7. During the allocation of marks, if any subjects need moderation marks more 
than the balance available in the semester, moderation marks will not be given. 
8. Any balance moderation marks available in a semester will not be carried over 
to next semester.
