The formal launch of TEN was initiated by Dr.Venu Srinivasan, MD, TVS Motors and Mr.Karumuttu T.Kannan, Chairman & Correspondent, TCE on 18/03/2017 TEN (TCE Entrepreneurs Network) EXPO The TCE Alumni Entrepreneurs are pioneers in Enterprising and doing well in their field of expertise. Around 65 Nos of TCE Entrepreneurs interested showcases their capabilities and products in the EXPO to celebrate the success of their Entrepreneurship journey in TCE during 29-30 September 2018. During this event, students of present batch  and alumni entrepreneurs gathered  at TCE and this event conceptualized to foster interaction between them and also  to motivate this  aspiring entrepreneur students.  This two day programme aims at showcasing a variety of lab facilities to companies across the country to provide deeper understanding about its infrastructure. This expo is inaugurated by S.Lakshmanan, Managing Director, Tecton Group of Companies, UAE and Usha Dixit, Scientist F from National  Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board ( NSTEDB), Government of India.
