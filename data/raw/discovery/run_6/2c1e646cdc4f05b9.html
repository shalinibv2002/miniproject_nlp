Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Vision and Mission

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) at Thiagarajar College of Engineering, established in 1957, has a distinguished legacy in education, research, and industry collaboration. Recognized with the AICTE-CII Best Industry-Linked Institute Award in 2014, the department is also supported by the DST under the FIST scheme, underscoring its research and infrastructure capabilities. The department's UG and PG programmes are accredited and re-accredited, with Outcome-Based Education (OBE) implemented since the academic year 2014–15 to ensure enhanced learning outcomes.

Our faculty members are the pillars of this legacy, having received prestigious honors such as the AICTE Career Award for Young Teachers (CAYT), TNSCST Young Scientist Fellowship (YSF), Post-Doctoral Research Fellowships, and national-level Educator Awards, reflecting their research excellence and academic contributions. The department actively fosters strong industry-academia ties through collaborations and curriculum enrichment initiatives with esteemed organizations including TVS Motors Limited, General Electric, Honeywell, CPRI, and CDAC. A notable collaboration is the MoU signed with TVS for the TCE-TVSM Collaborated Education Program (CEP), strengthening the department’s role in industry-relevant education and skill development.

Programmes Offered

 

Undergraduate Program:

 

B.E. Electrical and Electronics Engineering

The B.E. Electrical and Electronics Engineering program at Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) equips students with a strong foundation in electrical systems, electronics, and modern engineering practices. The course focuses on key areas such as power systems, electrical machines, control systems, electronics, embedded systems, renewable energy, IoT , Power electronics and smart grid technologies. With an emphasis on practical and project-based learning, the curriculum integrates design thinking and system thinking methodologies. Students engage in engineering design projects, capstone projects, and seminars to develop problem-solving skills and industry readiness.

B.E. Electrical and Computer Engineering

The B.E. Electrical and Computer Engineering programme at Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE) equips students with a strong foundation in electrical engineering, computing, and intelligent digital technologies. The curriculum seamlessly integrates electrical systems, electronics, computer programming, software engineering, computer organization and architecture, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, data communication and networks, Internet of Things (IoT), control systems, power electronics, renewable energy, smart grids, and cybersecurity. With a strong emphasis on experiential, CDIO-based, project-oriented, and interdisciplinary learning, students engage in engineering design, software–hardware co-design, firmware development, industry-focused use cases, capstone projects, internships, and innovation-driven activities to develop problem-solving, analytical, and leadership skills. The programme prepares graduates to design, develop, and deploy intelligent, secure, and sustainable engineering solutions, making them industry-ready for careers in electrical engineering, embedded and computer systems, software development, automation, semiconductor technologies, robotics, electric vehicles, smart infrastructure, and emerging digital industries.

 

Postgraduate Program:

 

M.E – Embedded System Technologies

The Embedded System Technologies programme provides students with a strong foundation in embedded hardware and software design, microcontrollers, real-time systems, IoT, and automation. The curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on learning to prepare graduates for the design and development of intelligent, reliable, and industry-ready embedded solutions.

 

Research Programs (Ph.D.) Offered by Thiagarajar College of Engineering

 

Thiagarajar College of Engineering provides comprehensive Ph.D. programs across various disciplines to promote cutting-edge research and innovation. In the field of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, research opportunities are offered in areas such as:

• Power Systems and Renewable Energy

• Electric Vehicles and Power Electronics

• Smart Grids and Energy Management

• Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence Applications in EEE

• IoT-based Systems

The Ph.D. program encourages interdisciplinary research, collaboration with industry, and real-world problem-solving, fostering advancements in technology and academic contributions.
