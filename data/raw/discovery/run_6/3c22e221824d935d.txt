Rooted in the institution’s philosophy of academic excellence, ethical values, innovation, and societal responsibility, the department prepares students to address the evolving needs of the global fashion and textile industry. Our curriculum emphasizes experiential learning, where classroom concepts are transformed into practical skills through design studios, advanced laboratories, industrial projects, live assignments, internships, and product development. Recognizing fashion as a global industry, the department fosters an international outlook through collaborations with leading universities, industry partners, research organizations, and professional bodies. With a strong focus on innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry engagement, the department equips graduates with the technical competence, creative confidence, and professional ethics required to become designers, technologists, researchers, entrepreneurs, and leaders capable of shaping the future of the fashion ecosystem. Programmes Offered The B.Tech. Fashion Technology programme integrates engineering principles, creative design, and technological innovation to prepare professionals for the dynamic global fashion industry. The programme aims to nurture graduates who are technically competent, industry-ready, and globally competitive. Designed around an experiential learning approach, the curriculum provides extensive hands-on training through well-equipped laboratories. Students will develop expertise across the entire fashion value chain, encompassing apparel design and engineering, manufacturing, quality assurance, merchandising, supply chain management, sustainability, and emerging digital technologies. The programme aspires to promote global exposure through collaborations with international universities, industry partners, research organizations, and professional bodies. With a strong emphasis on innovation, sustainability, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning, the programme aims to empower students to become future-ready professionals capable of creating responsible, value-driven solutions for the fashion and apparel sector.
