Description  
As per TCE IP Policy, a scrutiny meeting was conducted to examine the patentability and commercial 
viability of IDFs submitted by faculty and student members of TCE. The inventors of ten IDFs (Nos. IDF-
2022-011 to IDF -2022-020) have made a presentation to the committee. Based on the evaluation of 
external experts, the IDFs are classified into two categories. IDFs classified under category 1 are highly 
commercially viable and Patentable and category 2 are only Patentable. Since there is no IDFs are not 
classified under category 3, all ten IDFs can be submitted to IP office in the present form.
