PhD seats were allocated to 97 institutions (IITs, NITs, Central & State Universities etc.) in 25 states and 4 Union Territories. Now, 2nd phase of the scheme has been launched with budget of Rs. 481.93 Crores for 9 years targeting: 1000 Full Time PhD Candidates. 150 Part Time PhD Candidates. 50 awardees of Young Faculty Research Fellowship. 225 awardees of Post-Doctoral Fellowship. For further details, you can visit: https://phd.digitalindiacorporation.in Aim of the Scheme: The “Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme” aims to enhance the number of Ph.D. scholars in Information Technology (IT)/Electronics System Design & Manufacturing (ESDM) sectors and build strong research infrastructure in academic institutions. Target Sectors: IT/ITES (Information Technology Enabled Services) and ESDM (Electronic Design and Manufacturing) Research Domains: Emerging fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Quantum Computing, Wireless Networks, Smart Grid, Power Systems, Power Electronics, Drives, Machines, Renewable Integration, Micro-grids, Communications, Semiconductor, Antennas, VLSI Design, Image Processing, Signal Processing, Electromagnetics, RF & Microwaves, EV Interfacing, Cyber Security, Internet of Things, Robotics No. of seats available: 02 Full Time Ph.D Seats 01 Part Time Ph.D Seat Funding Support: Total Amount allocated: Rs.75 Lakhs for 2 Full Time and 1 Part Time Ph.D Candidate Grant for Part Time Ph.D: Fellowship @ Rs. 3 Lakhs on Ph.D. completion Grant for Full Time PhD: (Rs.72,61,600 for 2 Candidates) Details First two years Three - Five years Fellowship Rs.38,750/- per month Rs.43,750/- per month HRA As per Norms Institutional Overhead Rs.50000/- per year Contingency Grant Rs.2,40,000/- per year Project Duration 60 months or PhD completion of the enrolled Candidate(s), whichever is earlier Eligibility: PhD Regulations of Anna University (https://cfr.annauniv.edu/research/regulation/Ph.D-Regulation-2025.pdf) and Approved research domains of Electronic Design and Manufacturing (ESDM) and Information Technology Enabled Services (ITES), MeitY. Admission Process Call for applications through social media, and TCE Website Receiving the Applications (Applicant should select the GUIDE from TCE alone) Intimation to the eligible candidates for test and interview Verification of Certificates, Test, and Interview Announcement of the Eligible Candidates Approval (Centre for Research - Anna University, Affiliating University and Visveswaraya PhD cell, MeitY) Enrolment at Centre for Research - Anna University and TCE Apply through the following google form  : https://forms.gle/gkNcFhvvGVXZYUbm9 Last date of the application: May 30, 2025 Head of the Visvesvaraya Ph.D. Scheme: Dr L Ashok Kumar, Principal, TCE Nodal Officer of the Visvesvaraya Ph.D. Scheme:  Dr.S.Sridevi, Professor, Department of Information Technology, TCE For any clarifications, contact the Nodal Officer through the mail – sridevi@tce.edu Quick Links Mandatory Disclosure RTI _Act Statutory Committees Transcripts Help Desk Payments Terms & Conditions Explore Career Campus Life Institute Newsletter Campus Map TCE AICTE Idea Lab Thiagarajar College of Engineering Madurai - 625 015 Tamil Nadu, India +91 452 2482240 www.tce.edu TCE MOOCs | TCE Courses | TBI | Downloads | Contact
