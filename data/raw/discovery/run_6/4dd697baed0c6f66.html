Electronics and Communication Engineering

Vision and Mission

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, established in 1978, offers B.E., M.E., and Ph.D. Programs. Degrees, with NBA and NAAC A++ accreditation. It is a hub for innovation, hosting a TIFAC CORE in Wireless Technologies, with Industry and Nationally Supported labs. The department has secured 15+ patents and receives funding from top organizations like DRDO, DRDL, Meity, DIT, CDAC, ISRO, RCI, NPMASS, AICTE, DST, and UGC. Known for strong industry ties, it boasts an excellent placement record in leading core companies such as Honeywell, Multicoreware, EmbedUR, Einfochips, and Amphenol Omniconnect India Pvt Ltd.

Highlights:

DST-FIST supported TIFAC CORE in Wireless Technologies and Antenna Testing Facility (up to 18 GHz). Incubated Thiagarajar Telekom Solutions Pvt. Ltd. and secured five international patents. Received AICTE CII Best Industry Linked Institution award and CISCO Networking Academy Award. Granted with sponsored projects from MeitY, DRDO, and ISRO. Active professional societies student chapters, such as IEEE, IEI, and received the IEEE Student Branch Award 2024.

Programmes Offered

 

Undergraduate Program:

 

B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering

Established in 1978, this programme provides students with a robust foundation in electronics and communication systems, emphasizing theoretical knowledge and practical skills. It integrates industry-oriented training and exposure to emerging technologies to prepare graduates for innovative careers in core and interdisciplinary fields.

B.Tech. Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) Degree Programme

The B.Tech. Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) programme provides students with a strong foundation in semiconductor technology, integrated circuit design, and advanced electronic systems. The curriculum covers CMOS VLSI, ASIC/FPGA design, embedded systems, RISC-V architecture, and System-on-Chip (SoC) design, with emphasis on EDA tools, design verification, DFT, and low-power VLSI. Through modern laboratories, industry-oriented training, projects, internships, and research, the programme prepares graduates for careers in semiconductor design, embedded systems, R&D, and technology entrepreneurship.

Postgraduate Program:

 

M.E. Communication Systems

Started in 1995, this programme focuses on advanced learning in areas like wireless communication, signal processing, and optical networks. It equips students with specialized skills to address modern engineering challenges, fostering leadership in research, development, and industry.

Research Program:

 

Ph.D. in Electronics and Communication Engineering

Introduced in 2001, this doctoral programme encourages innovative research in cutting-edge areas such as VLSI design, embedded systems, and wireless technologies. It empowers scholars to contribute to academic and industrial advancements by addressing complex technological challenges.
