Intellectual Property Rights Activity 
 
The Industry Institute Interaction Cell of our college has jointly organized a five days workshop  with 
Centre for Intellectual Property Rights (CIPR) , Anna University, Chennai on “Certificate Course on Effective Patent 
Search and Drafting - PAT DRAFT - 2019” from 25th November 2019 to 29 th November 2019 at ECE Dept. Seminar 
hall. The objective of the workshop is to discuss about their novel ideas and get the feedback from resource person 
whether the idea is innovative for patentable. 23 participants have attended and benefited with this workshop.
