IPR Cell

Overview

IP (Intellectual Property) refers to the intangible creations resulting from human intellect, including patents, copyright, trademarks, industrial design rights, and trade secrets. Intellectual property provides a new way for an institution to disseminate its knowledge and creative solutions for the societal and industrial needs. One of the missions of the TCE is to develop the intellectual capability to its fullest potential. TCE has a separate IPR Cell under Research and Development Team. 

Objectives

The Intellectual Property Cell aims to facilitate, encourage, promote, and protect the intellectual property rights of members of the faculty, students, research scholars, and staff at TCE.
