Intellectual Property Rights Activity 
 
The Industry Institute Interaction Cell of our college has organized a One Day Workshop on “ Myths and 
Realities of R&D and IPR at EEIs: Issues, Challenges & Opportunities ” held on 13th July 2018 between 9.15AM to 
4.15PM at Mechanical Seminar Hall .Resource Person Dr. K. Gopalakrishnan  delivered a talk on  Issues, Challenges 
and Opportunities in IPR  and R&D. The objective of the workshop is to explore the opportunity for converting our 
research and project work into patent. 50 participants have attended and benefited with this workshop.
