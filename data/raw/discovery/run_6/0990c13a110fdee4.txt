Intellectual Property Rights Activity- Patent filing procedure in India and 
Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2021 
 
 
IPR cell of TCE organized a IP awareness programme on “ Patent filing 
procedure in India and Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2021 ” 
on 13th November 2021  (Saturday) from  10.30 A.M. to 12 Noon  for all our 
faculty members, research scholars and students of TCE.  This webinar was 
handled by Mr. Ram Jawahar, Assistant Controller of Patents and Designs, Indian 
Patent Office, Chennai. The focus of the programme was  “how to file patent in 
India and the new patent amendment rules 2021 ”. Totally, 112 participants 
including 27 faculty and 85 students attended the programme and got benefitted.
