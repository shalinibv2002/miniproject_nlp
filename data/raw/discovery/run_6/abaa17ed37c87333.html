INDUSTRY & ENTREPRENEURSHIP
  • TCE’s Technology Business Incubator (TBI) nurtured 12 startups, including EVVI Solutions and Aura MEP, through infrastructure and mentorship.
  • The Institution Innovation Council (IIC) signed 29 MoUs with industries and raised ₹99.58 lakh in consultancy revenue.
  • Hosted MSME Hackathon 4.0, granting ₹15 lakh for student innovations.
  • Centers like TSSCAR and CEEPT offer industrial training, prototyping, and testing support, bridging academia and industry.
  • Regular collaboration with Thoughtworks, Amazon, BHEL, and IIT Bombay enables real-time exposure to emerging technologies.

 

RANKING & RECOGNITION
  • NAAC A++ Accreditation (CGPA 3.56/4.00) in Cycle II and NBA Accredited Programmes, reinforcing quality education standards.
  • NIRF 2025 Rankings: Engineering in the 101–150 band
  • NIRF 2025 Rankings: Architecture and Planning ranked 33rd in India
  • Featured in Times Higher Education (THE) Rankings 2025, 2026 across multiple bands
  • Featured in QS World University Rankings: Asia 2026
  • 4th position in T-Schools DataQuest Award 2025 and  AAAA+ Rating from Careers 360 in 2024
  • Excellence in Innovation & IIC Best Practices Award at the IIC Southern Regional Meet 2025 More Details

 

RESEARCH & INNOVATION
  • Awarded 589 PhDs to date; 68 scholars supported under Thiagarajar Research Fellowship (TRF).
  • Achieved 1,595 WoS and 4,432 Scopus-indexed publications with 42 design patents filed.
  • Strong global collaborations with DRDO, IITs, CIPET, and University of Nottingham.
  • Centres of Excellence like MuthirAI (AI in Tamil) and AWS, IBM & Bentley CoEs fuel interdisciplinary research and innovation.

 

PLACEMENTS & INTERNSHIPS
  • Highest package: ₹28 LPA and average package: ₹7.25 LPA during the academic year 2024–25.
  • Over 100 recruiters, including 25+ core engineering companies and 20+ super dream companies, hired students across diverse domains.
  • 345 students secured internships at leading organizations such as KLA Tencor, HPE Aruba, Caterpillar, Contentstack, and Texas Instruments, providing valuable real-world exposure and industry experience.
  • The highest internship stipend reached ₹1 lakh per month, reflecting the strong industry demand for our students.
  • The curriculum is closely aligned with industry requirements, complemented by MOOCs, Coursera, and NPTEL certifications to enhance students’ technical and professional competencies.
