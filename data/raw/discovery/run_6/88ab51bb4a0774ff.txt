TCE and TVS Motor had jointly developed an industry supported curriculum program for the students. TSS CAR was established in collaboration with M/s TVS Motor Company, Hosur at Thiagarajar College of Engineering, Madurai with an initial investment of Rs.11 Crores and Built-up area -16,400 sq.ft. The Centre was inaugurated by Mr.Venu Srinivasan, Managing Director, TVS Motor Company, on 29th November 2019 in the presence of Mr. Karumuttu T. Kannan, Chairman, Thiagarajar College of Engineering. Mission of TSSCAR To provide an interdisciplinary and collaborative environment for new product development. To enable consultancy, training and testing projects to support industry needs.
