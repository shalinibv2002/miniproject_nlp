Sports

Department of Physical Education

Since 1957, this department has been successfully functioning. The department is encouraging the students to participate in various sports and games in the international /national /state /university /district levels.The department has been producing tremendous results in the sports and games.

Objectives

  • To train the students to become healthy, refine their sports, games skills and develop leadership qualities
  • To identify and nurture the sports talents of the students and coach them systematically to become a champion
  • To create excellent sports infrastructure and promote new sports and games
