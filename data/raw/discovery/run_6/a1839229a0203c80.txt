PAYING EXAMINATIONS FEES ONLINE THROUGH EAZYPAY (ICICI BANK) 
1. Open URL: https://eazypay.icicibank.com/ 
2. Type T C E (Provide space between each Alphabet) in the space for typing the 
name of the institution. 
 
3. Choose T C E AUTONOMOUS ACCOUNT 
4. Enter the mobile number (any of your mobile number to which the OTP has to 
be sent), Reg number (Ex:17AR0XX/ 20E0XX) and Captcha 
 
5. In student Name box: Type your name first then provide space then type initial (all in 
capital letters) 
6. Enter the OTP received. 
7. The bill will be found for payment
