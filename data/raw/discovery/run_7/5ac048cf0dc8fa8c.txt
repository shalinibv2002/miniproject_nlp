It aims to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry demands through various activities, workshops, and events. The association organizes technical seminars, coding competitions, hackathons, and guest lectures by industry experts to provide students with exposure to the latest trends in technology. It also encourages collaborative learning through peer-driven projects, technical clubs, and mentoring programs. The club serves as a platform for students to sharpen their analytical and programming skills through regular coding challenges, algorithm workshops, and mock contests. Algo Geeks Club is not just about coding but also about creating a collaborative space where ideas are shared, innovation is encouraged, and problem-solving becomes a way of life. AR/VR Club The AR/VR Club is a creative and technology-driven community focused on exploring the immersive worlds of Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR). This club provides a platform for students to learn, innovate, and develop cutting-edge applications that blend the physical and digital realms. The club hosts hands-on workshops, tech talks to introduce members to AR/VR tools, frameworks, and programming languages like Unity, Unreal Engine, and WebXR. Students work on exciting projects, ranging from immersive games and virtual tours to educational simulations and real-world AR applications.
