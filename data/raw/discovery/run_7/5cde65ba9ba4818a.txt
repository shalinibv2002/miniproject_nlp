Machine mail is our Department’s official magazine edited and presented by a team of skilled MEA members. This magazine portrays our Department’s austerity in various domains including technical articles, art, craft, sports, and social service along with our staff accomplishments. For this academic year, we will be releasing 2 different versions under the same banner. To increase, the enthusiasm and competitiveness among the students we have divided the entire department into 4 teams namely Carina, Orion, Draco, and Bellatrix. Points will be awarded to the teams based on participation and the number of winners. The team with the highest point at the end of the academic year will be awarded with a trophy.
