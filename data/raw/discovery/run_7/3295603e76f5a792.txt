Kannan, Chairman, Thiagarajar College of Engineering. • TSSCAR was started with initial investment of Rs.11 Crores with a Built-up area of -16,400 sq. ft Mission of TSSCAR • To provide an interdisciplinary and collaborative environment for new product development. • To enable consultancy, training and testing projects to support industry needs. • To enable academic and sponsored research in the area of new product development.
