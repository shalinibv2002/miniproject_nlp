Student chapter of IEI has been functioning for more than 10 years and regularly conducting technical events including design contest , mini project contest, technical quiz ,interdisciplinary lab visits etc., Participation in National Level NDRF –IE design contest 2 Society of Automotive Engineers, India (SAE) SAE INDIA, an affiliate of Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) International, is the premier professional society dedicated to advancing mobility engineering in India and has a vision to continuously enrich knowledge base of practitioners in mobility industry and institutions in the service of humanity. SAE Collegiate Club of TCE was inaugurated on 12 August 2008 by the initiation taken by the department of Mechanical Engineering. SAE Student Convention is being conducted every year with the support from SAE INDIA in three tiers. TIER-I level events are conducted at college level. Winners in the TIER-I events will be representing in TIER-II (Zonal Level) and TIER-III (Section Level) .The various events conducted yearly in the student convention are:Paper Presentation: Students present research papers on automotive and mechanical engineering topics. CAD Modeling and Drafting: Competitions focusing on computer-aided design skills. Business Plan Competition: Teams develop and present entrepreneurial business plans. Auto Quiz: A quiz competition testing knowledge in automotive engineering. Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM): Competitions involving manufacturing process simulations. CAE Contest: Competitions focusing on computer-aided engineering analysis. Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering Aero Modeling: Design and demonstration of model aircraft. Technical Poster Presentation: Visual presentation of engineering projects and research. Project Display: Showcasing of student-engineered projects and prototypes. 3 Indian Society of Heating, Refrigeration and Air conditioning Engineers (ISHRAE): Sub chapter Inaugurated in 2013 Inculcating the future of HVAC to the students by arranging Workshop and one credit course , contest. ISHRAE student chapters in more than 150 engineering colleges encourage students to opt for careers in the HVAC&R industry. Knowledge dissemination is done through seminars, quiz contests like aQuest, plant and site visits. ISHRAE has launched “ISHRAE Job Junction” nationally, providing a platform for leading employers to recruit candidates who are members from ISHRAE student chapters. K-12 initiative of ISHRAE is focused on school students’ contests, in making them aware of subjects like, energy conservation and environmental concerns through drawing competitions, poster design, quiz and planting of trees. Emphasis on STEM education is stressed to inculcate a scientific fervor & help develop these young children into responsible citizens.
