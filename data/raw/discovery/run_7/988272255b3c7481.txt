The function begins with the prayer song and lighting of kuthuvilaku. The function commenced with a warm and heartfelt welcome address by Ms.M.SANKARI, General Secretary, Final Year. Followed by the HOD ADDRESS by Dr.C.K.BABULAL sir, addressed the audience focusing on the department's role in shaping students' academic journeys. The Presidential Address was delivered by Dr.M.PALANINATHARAJA sir. The inaugural address was delivered by Mr.s.R.M.KABILA, the public and motivation speaker and Alumni TCE Madurai. During the Inaugural Function, a special moment was dedicated to honor and appreciate the esteemed Chief Guest, Mr.s.R.M.KABILA. The Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) Association Address was given by Mr.V.R.ANANDHA KRISHNAN, General Secretary, Final Year The Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) Association Activities Address was given by Mr.R.ABBAS, General Secretary, Final Year The IEEE Association Activities and IEI Association Activities Address was given by Mr.R.AADITHYA, General Chair(IEEE), Final Year and Ms.R.DEEPASHRI, General coordinator (IEI). The event concluded with a vote of thanks by Ms.P.GAYATHRI, General Secretary, Final Year.
