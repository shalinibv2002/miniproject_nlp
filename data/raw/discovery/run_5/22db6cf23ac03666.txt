He was entrusted with the moral and social responsibility of building this institution with the objective of serving society. A staunch nationalist and a remarkable industrialist, he led a life of sacrifice. He was also an exceptional educationalist, with a keen focus on transforming lives through education. As a prominent social reformer, he left an indelible mark on all aspects of social life. A devoted reader, his passion for language was evident in his profound command of Tamil. As a generous philanthropist through his righteous deeds, he continues to live in the hearts of many. Kalaithanthai Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar had a holistic personality that radiated spiritual aura and wisdom.
